Production workflow
01Discovery call
Understanding your product, audience, and goals. Aligning on positioning, messaging, and the video's core idea before anything else.
02Creative Direction
Defining the overall style, pacing, and audio direction. Locking the rhythm and format early, before a single frame is designed.
03Script
A concise script focused on clarity and impact. Every line written to support the product story, nothing extra, nothing missing.
04Design
A detailed storyboard in Figma with real-time access and frame-by-frame visibility into structure and flow before animation begins.
05Animation
After Effects production with Frame.io reviews. Comment on exact timestamps, suggest changes, and follow progress clearly throughout.
06Final Delivery
All formats (16:9, 5:4, 9:16), After Effects and Figma source files, packaged and handed over cleanly.

For fully animated videos, pricing is based on the full final duration because the entire video is designed and animated. For footage + motion videos, pricing is split into full-video post-production at $15/s and motion graphics billed only for the actual animated sections. Format adaptations are billed at $10/s per selected version. Hook variations apply only to fully animated videos and are billed at main rate × 5s per hook. Rush delivery fees apply to the full project scope. All rates are confirmed in writing before production begins. Delivery timelines are in business days, Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays, and begin from the project kickoff date.
